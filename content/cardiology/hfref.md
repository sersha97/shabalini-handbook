:::bottom
- **HFrEF = EF ≤40%.** Four pillars: **ARNI · β-blocker · MRA · SGLT2i**. Together they cut mortality by ~60%.
- **Start all four early**, even at low doses. Order matters less than speed.
- Ischemic workup for every new cardiomyopathy.
- **ICD** if EF ≤35% after ≥90 days of GDMT (≥40 days after MI). **CRT** if LBBB + QRS ≥150.
- **HFimpEF** (EF improved >40%) → **never stop GDMT**.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Injured myocardium (MI, toxins, genetic, myocarditis, tachycardia) → low stroke volume.
- The body "defends" BP with **RAAS + sympathetic drive + vasopressin** → short-term help, long-term harm: fibrosis, remodeling, dilation.
- **Each pillar blocks a harmful neurohormone** (ARNI, BB, MRA) or unloads the heart and kidney (SGLT2i).

## 🔎 Classification

| Scheme | Levels |
|---|---|
| **ACC/AHA stages** | A at risk · B structural disease, no symptoms · C symptoms · D advanced |
| **NYHA class** | I none · II ordinary activity · III less than ordinary · IV at rest |
| **EF** | HFrEF ≤40 · HFmrEF 41–49 · HFpEF ≥50 · HFimpEF (was ≤40, now >40) |

:::new 2026 update
The **Second Universal Definition of HF** (June 2026) moves away from rigid EF cut-offs and stresses cause, trajectory and early detection. Treatment still follows the 2022 guideline until the new AHA/ACC guideline (expected 2027).
:::

## 🧪 Workup — new cardiomyopathy

TTE · ECG · BNP · BMP, CBC, TSH, iron, A1c, lipids, HIV/hepatitis (selected), **ischemic evaluation** (cath or CCTA) · cardiac MRI (myocarditis, sarcoid, amyloid, LV thrombus) · alcohol, cocaine, chemo history · genetic testing if family history.

## 💊 Management — the 4 pillars

| Drug | Start | Target | Watch |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sacubitril/valsartan** | 24/26 mg BID (49/51 if on ACEi/ARB) | 97/103 mg BID | **36 h washout** after ACEi; BP, K, Cr; angioedema |
| **Carvedilol** | 3.125 mg BID | 25 mg BID (50 if >85 kg) | Not in decompensation/shock |
| **Metoprolol succinate** | 12.5–25 mg daily | 200 mg daily | Only succinate (not tartrate) has HF data |
| **Spironolactone** | 12.5–25 mg daily | 25–50 mg daily | K <5.0, eGFR >30; gynecomastia → eplerenone |
| **Dapagliflozin / empagliflozin** | 10 mg daily | 10 mg daily | Genital infection; hold if NPO/sick (euglycemic DKA) |

:::pearl
**Soft BP?** Start SGLT2i and MRA first (little BP effect), then low-dose BB and ARNI. A small Cr rise (≤30%) after ARNI/MRA/SGLT2i is expected — do not stop.
:::

:::tabs
=== Add-ons
- **Hydralazine + isosorbide dinitrate** — self-identified Black patients, NYHA III–IV on GDMT (A-HeFT); or anyone who cannot take ARNI/ACEi/ARB.
- **Ivabradine** — sinus rhythm, HR ≥70 on max-tolerated BB.
- **Vericiguat** — recent worsening HF.
- **Digoxin** — persistent symptoms; target level 0.5–0.9.
- **IV iron** (ferric carboxymaltose / derisomaltose) — ferritin <100, or 100–299 + TSAT <20%.
- **Loop diuretic** — for congestion only (symptoms, not survival).
=== Devices
- **ICD (primary prevention):** EF ≤35%, NYHA II–III, ≥90 days GDMT, ≥40 days post-MI, life expectancy >1 year.
- **CRT:** EF ≤35%, sinus, **LBBB with QRS ≥150 ms** (strongest benefit).
- **TEER (MitraClip):** severe secondary MR despite GDMT (COAPT).
- **LifeVest** — bridge in selected new cardiomyopathy.
=== Avoid
- Non-DHP CCB (diltiazem, verapamil) · NSAIDs · TZDs (pioglitazone) · dronedarone · most antiarrhythmics except amiodarone/dofetilide · saxagliptin.
:::

## ⚠️ Pitfalls

- One drug titrated to max while others never start.
- Stopping BB in stable patients for mild hypotension or fatigue.
- Forgetting the 36-hour ACEi → ARNI washout.
- Stopping GDMT when EF recovers (TRED-HF: relapse).

## 🎓 Board corner

| Stem | Answer |
|---|---|
| EF 30%, on lisinopril, switch to ARNI | Stop ACEi, wait 36 h |
| EF 30% on GDMT, NYHA II, QRS 160 LBBB | CRT-D |
| Which BB is NOT HFrEF evidence-based? | Metoprolol **tartrate**, atenolol |
| Black patient, NYHA III on full GDMT | Add hydralazine–ISDN |
| HFrEF + gout → NSAID? | Avoid NSAIDs |
| EF improved 30% → 50% on GDMT | Continue all GDMT |
