:::bottom
- **Pericarditis = 2 of 4:** pleuritic chest pain better leaning forward · friction rub · diffuse STE/PR depression · new/worse effusion.
- **NSAID (ibuprofen) + colchicine 3 months.** Colchicine halves recurrence.
- **Avoid early steroids** (more recurrence) unless contraindication to NSAIDs or specific cause.
- **Admit** if fever >38 °C, large effusion, tamponade, no response to NSAIDs in 1 week, troponin ↑ (myopericarditis), immunosuppressed, on anticoagulants, trauma.
- **Tamponade = clinical diagnosis** (hypotension, JVD, pulsus) + echo → **drain** (pericardiocentesis). Fluids only buy time.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Inflamed pericardial layers rub → sharp pleuritic pain, rub, **epicardial** injury current → **diffuse** concave STE.
- **Tamponade:** fluid fills the fixed sac → pressure rises → right heart cannot fill in diastole → **low preload → low output**.
- **Speed matters more than volume:** 150 mL fast (trauma, rupture) can kill; 1 L slow (hypothyroid) may be tolerated.
- With inspiration the RV fills more and pushes the septum left → LV fills less → **pulsus paradoxus**.

## 🔎 Diagnosis

{{ecg:pericarditis|Diffuse concave ST elevation with PR depression (and PR elevation in aVR). No reciprocal ST depression (except aVR, V1).}}

| Feature | Pericarditis | STEMI |
|---|---|---|
| ST shape | Concave, diffuse | Convex, territorial |
| Reciprocal STD | No (except aVR) | Yes |
| PR depression | Yes | No |
| Q waves | No | Can develop |

**Causes:** idiopathic/viral (~85%) · post-MI / post-cardiac surgery · uremic · autoimmune · TB (endemic areas) · malignancy · drugs · radiation.

**Labs:** CRP (guides treatment length), troponin, CBC, BMP; TTE for all. Targeted: ANA, TSH, TB, HIV, blood cultures.

## 💊 Management

| Drug | Dose | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| **Ibuprofen** | 600–800 mg q8h + PPI | 1–2 weeks, then taper by symptoms/CRP |
| Aspirin (post-MI, CAD) | 750–1000 mg q8h | 1–2 weeks, taper |
| **Colchicine** | 0.5 mg BID (0.5 mg daily if <70 kg) | **3 months** (6 months recurrent) |
| Prednisone (2nd line) | 0.2–0.5 mg/kg/day | Slow taper |
| Rilonacept / anakinra | Recurrent, CRP-positive, steroid-dependent | Specialist |

- **Uremic pericarditis** → dialysis.
- Restrict strenuous exercise until symptoms resolve and CRP normal (athletes ≥3 months).

## 🫀 Tamponade

| Sign | Note |
|---|---|
| **Beck's triad** | Hypotension, JVD, muffled heart sounds |
| **Pulsus paradoxus** | SBP drops >10 mmHg on inspiration |
| Tachycardia | Almost always |
| ECG | Low voltage, **electrical alternans** |
| Echo | **RA systolic collapse, RV diastolic collapse**, plethoric IVC, respiratory variation of mitral inflow >25% |

:::warn Do not
- Give diuretics or vasodilators (cut preload further).
- Intubate before drainage if you can avoid it (positive pressure → arrest).
:::

- **Treat:** pericardiocentesis (echo-guided). Surgical window for recurrent, loculated, purulent, or hemorrhagic (aortic dissection/free wall rupture — **no needle** in dissection → surgery).
- Temporize: IV fluid bolus, norepinephrine.

## 🎓 Board corner

| Stem | Answer |
|---|---|
| Post-viral chest pain better leaning forward, diffuse STE | Ibuprofen + colchicine |
| Same + troponin elevated | Myopericarditis → admit, limit exercise |
| Hypotension, JVD, pulsus 18 mmHg | Tamponade → pericardiocentesis |
| Electrical alternans | Large effusion swinging |
| Kussmaul sign, pericardial knock, calcified pericardium | Constrictive pericarditis |
| Weeks after MI: fever, pleuritic pain, effusion | Dressler syndrome |
