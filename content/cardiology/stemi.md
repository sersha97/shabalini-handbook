:::bottom
- **ECG within 10 minutes** of chest pain. STEMI or equivalent → **activate the cath lab**.
- **Primary PCI:** first medical contact → device **≤90 min** (≤120 min if transfer).
- PCI not possible in 120 min → **fibrinolysis ≤30 min** (tenecteplase), then transfer for cath within 2–24 h.
- **Aspirin 162–325 mg chewed + P2Y12 (ticagrelor or prasugrel) + heparin.**
- **High-intensity statin.** LDL goal <70, consider <55 (2025).
- Avoid nitrates in **RV infarct**, hypotension, or recent PDE-5 inhibitor.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Plaque **ruptures or erodes** → platelet + fibrin thrombus → **complete occlusion** of an epicardial artery → transmural ischemia → ST elevation.
- "Time is muscle": necrosis spreads from endocardium to epicardium over ~3–6 h.
- The goal is not the ECG — it is the **occluded artery** (the "OMI" concept). Some occlusions do not meet STE criteria.

## 🔎 Diagnosis

{{ecg:stemi|New ST elevation at the J point in 2 contiguous leads.}}

| Leads | Threshold (new STE at J point) |
|---|---|
| V2–V3 | ≥2 mm men ≥40 · ≥2.5 mm men <40 · ≥1.5 mm women |
| All other leads | ≥1 mm |

| STEMI equivalents | Look for |
|---|---|
| **Posterior MI** | ST depression V1–V3 (tall R) → posterior leads V7–V9 ≥0.5 mm |
| **LBBB / paced** | **Smith-modified Sgarbossa:** concordant STE ≥1 mm, concordant STD V1–V3, or discordant STE/S ratio ≥25% |
| **de Winter T waves** | Upsloping STD + tall symmetric T in precordial leads (proximal LAD) |
| **aVR elevation + diffuse STD** | Left main / 3-vessel ischemia (or global subendocardial) |
| **Wellens** (pain-free) | Deep or biphasic T in V2–V3 → critical LAD; do **not** stress test |
| **Inferior STE** | Get **V4R** (RV infarct: STE ≥1 mm) |

:::warn STE mimics
Pericarditis · early repolarization · LVH · LV aneurysm · hyperkalemia · Brugada · Takotsubo · LBBB. Compare with old ECGs and repeat every 15–30 min.
:::

## 💊 Management

:::tabs
=== First 30 minutes
- **Aspirin** 162–325 mg chewed → 81 mg daily.
- **P2Y12:** ticagrelor 180 mg → 90 mg BID, **or** prasugrel 60 mg → 10 mg daily (avoid with prior stroke/TIA; 5 mg if <60 kg; generally not ≥75 y). Clopidogrel 600 mg if lytics or high bleed risk.
- **Anticoagulant:** UFH 70–100 U/kg bolus (in lab per protocol).
- O₂ only if SpO₂ <90%. Nitroglycerin SL for pain (not in RV MI/hypotension/PDE-5). Opioid if needed.
- **High-intensity statin** (atorvastatin 80 mg).
=== Reperfusion
- **Primary PCI** preferred. **Radial access.**
- Lytic (tenecteplase weight-based) if PCI >120 min away and symptoms <12 h. Then cath 2–24 h; rescue PCI if failed (STE <50% resolution at 60–90 min).
- **Non-culprit lesions:** complete revascularization in stable patients (staged or same procedure) — **not** in cardiogenic shock (culprit-only).
=== After PCI
- **DAPT 12 months** (most). High bleeding risk → shorter DAPT / de-escalation (e.g., ticagrelor monotherapy after 1 month).
- β-blocker if EF ≤40%, angina, arrhythmia. Routine BB in preserved EF after full revascularization: benefit uncertain (REDUCE-AMI, REBOOT).
- ACEi/ARB if EF ≤40%, HTN, DM, CKD. **MRA** if EF ≤40% + HF or DM.
- LDL goal <70 (consider <55): statin → add **ezetimibe** → PCSK9 inhibitor.
- **Cardiac rehab** referral. Smoking cessation.
- LV thrombus risk (large anterior MI) → echo; anticoagulate if thrombus.
:::

## ⚠️ Complications timeline

| When | Complication | Clue |
|---|---|---|
| Hours | VF/VT, AV block, cardiogenic shock | Telemetry |
| Days 2–7 | **Papillary muscle rupture** (acute MR) | New murmur, flash edema |
| Days 3–5 | **VSR** | Holosystolic murmur, step-up O₂ sat RA → RV |
| Days 5–14 | **Free wall rupture** | Sudden tamponade, PEA |
| Days 1–3 | Peri-infarct pericarditis | Pleuritic pain, rub |
| Weeks | Dressler syndrome, LV aneurysm (persistent STE), LV thrombus | |

## 🎓 Board corner

| Stem | Answer |
|---|---|
| Inferior STEMI, BP 80/50 after nitro, clear lungs | RV infarct → fluids, no nitrates |
| ST depression V1–V3, tall R in V2 | Posterior MI → V7–V9 |
| Pain-free, deep T inversions V2–V3 | Wellens → cath, no stress test |
| Nearest PCI 3 h away, onset 2 h ago | Fibrinolysis, then transfer |
| Prasugrel in patient with prior stroke | Contraindicated |
| Day 5, sudden PEA arrest | Free wall rupture |
