:::bottom
- **Target 140–180 mg/dL** for most inpatients (100–180 in selected stable patients).
- **Sliding scale alone is not a plan.** Use **basal + nutritional (prandial) + correction** (RABBIT-2).
- **Starting total daily dose (TDD): 0.3–0.5 U/kg** (0.2–0.3 if age >70, eGFR <30, low weight); **50% basal, 50% prandial**.
- **NPO → reduce basal by ~20–25%**, stop prandial, keep correction.
- **Prednisone in the morning → NPH in the morning** (0.1 U/kg per 10 mg prednisone, max 0.4 U/kg).
- Every hypoglycemia (<70) → review and **reduce** the regimen the same day.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Illness = **stress hormones** (cortisol, catecholamines, cytokines) → insulin resistance → hyperglycemia even without known diabetes ("stress hyperglycemia").
- Hyperglycemia → infection, worse wound healing, longer stay. **Hypoglycemia** → arrhythmia, falls, death — just as harmful.
- Insulin needs change daily (eating, steroids, infection improving) → **adjust every day**.

## 🔎 Diagnosis

- Check **A1c** if none in the last 3 months (A1c ≥6.5% → diabetes; also guides discharge).
- POC glucose: before meals + bedtime (eating) or q4–6h (NPO/continuous feeds).
- Type 1 diabetes → **always basal insulin**, even NPO (risk of DKA).

## 💊 Management

:::tabs
=== Basal-bolus math
Example: 80 kg, eating, eGFR normal → TDD 0.4 × 80 = **32 U**
- Basal: **16 U glargine** daily
- Prandial: **16 U ÷ 3 ≈ 5 U lispro** with each meal (hold if eating <50%)
- Correction: sensitive / usual / resistant scale with meals

Titrate basal by 10–20% every 1–2 days to fasting target.
=== Special situations
| Situation | Plan |
|---|---|
| **NPO** | Basal 75–80% of dose; correction q4–6h; no prandial |
| **Continuous tube feeds** | Basal 40% of TDD + regular insulin q6h or NPH q8–12h for the feed; **if feeds stop → start D10** |
| **Prednisone (AM)** | **NPH with the steroid** (0.1 U/kg per 10 mg prednisone, max 0.4 U/kg) |
| Dexamethasone | Longer acting → basal (glargine) increase or NPH BID |
| Type 2, mild hyperglycemia, eating little | Basal + correction (or DPP-4 inhibitor + correction in selected) |
| Insulin pump / CGM | May continue if patient is alert and able, with policy |
=== Hypoglycemia
- <70: 15–20 g fast carbs (or D50 25 mL IV if NPO/AMS, glucagon 1 mg IM if no IV) → recheck in 15 min.
- Find why: missed meal with prandial given, renal decline, steroids tapered, sepsis.
- Reduce the regimen **10–20%** (or more).
=== Oral agents in hospital
- **Metformin:** hold if contrast with AKI, eGFR <30, hypoxia, sepsis, liver failure.
- **SGLT2i:** hold if NPO, acutely ill, or 3 days before surgery (euglycemic DKA).
- **GLP-1 RA:** continue if eating; hold with vomiting/pancreatitis concerns.
- Sulfonylureas: usually stop (hypoglycemia).
:::

## 🏠 Discharge

| A1c | Plan |
|---|---|
| <8% | Home regimen (or optimize) |
| 8–10% | Home oral agents + basal (~50% of inpatient basal) |
| >10% | Basal-bolus or basal + GLP-1 RA / intensify |

- [ ] Glucometer, strips, pen needles, teaching (teach-back)
- [ ] Hypoglycemia plan; check insurance coverage of insulin
- [ ] SGLT2i/GLP-1 RA if ASCVD, HF, CKD
- [ ] PCP/endocrine follow-up in 1–2 weeks

## ⚠️ Pitfalls

- "Sliding scale only" for days.
- Full prandial doses when the patient is not eating.
- Missing the afternoon/evening spike from AM prednisone.
- Stopping feeds without a dextrose plan → hypoglycemia.

## 🎓 Board corner

| Stem | Answer |
|---|---|
| Type 1 diabetes, NPO for surgery | Continue basal (reduced), never stop |
| Glucose high only in afternoon on prednisone | AM NPH |
| Inpatient target in non-critically ill | 140–180 |
| Hypoglycemia at 3 AM, fasting high | Somogyi is rare — check basal dose and timing |
