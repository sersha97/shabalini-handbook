:::bottom
- **Emergency surgery → go.** Optimize on the way; do not delay for testing.
- Active cardiac condition (ACS, decompensated HF, severe valve disease, unstable arrhythmia) → **fix it first**.
- **Low risk (RCRI 0–1 / <1% MACE) → proceed.**
- **Elevated risk + good functional capacity (≥4 METs, e.g., 2 flights of stairs) → proceed.**
- Elevated risk + poor/unknown capacity → **BNP/NT-proBNP** (2024) → stress test **only if it would change management** anyway.
- "Cardiac clearance" does not exist — write **risk + recommendations**.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Surgery = stress test: catecholamines, bleeding, hypotension, hypercoagulability, anemia, pain.
- Most perioperative MIs are **type 2 (supply–demand)**, silent, and found only with troponin → **MINS** (myocardial injury after non-cardiac surgery) predicts 30-day death.
- Pre-op revascularization of stable CAD does **not** reduce risk (CARP).

## 🔎 Risk tools

| RCRI (1 point each) |
|---|
| High-risk surgery (intraperitoneal, intrathoracic, suprainguinal vascular) |
| Ischemic heart disease |
| Heart failure |
| Cerebrovascular disease |
| Insulin-treated diabetes |
| Creatinine >2.0 mg/dL |

0 → ~4% · 1 → ~6% · 2 → ~10% · ≥3 → ~15% (MACE in modern cohorts). Alternatives: **NSQIP calculator**, Gupta MICA.

- **≥4 METs** (2 flights of stairs, brisk walk 4 mph, heavy housework) = adequate. **DASI** score >34 more objective.
- **BNP ≥92 pg/mL or NT-proBNP ≥300** → higher risk → consider post-op troponin surveillance.
- ECG: elevated-risk surgery with known heart disease. **TTE**: unexplained dyspnea, murmur, HF change.

## 💊 Medication plan

:::tabs
=== Cardiac drugs
| Drug | Plan |
|---|---|
| **β-blocker** | **Continue** if chronic. Do **not** start on the day of surgery (POISE). |
| Statin | Continue; start if indicated |
| ACEi/ARB | Continue or hold 24 h — individualize (hold if hypotension risk); restart early |
| SGLT2 inhibitor | **Hold 3 days** (ertugliflozin 4 days) — euglycemic DKA |
| Aspirin (secondary prevention) | Continue for most; hold for closed-space surgery (spine, intracranial, posterior eye) |
| Aspirin (primary prevention) | Hold 7 days |
| P2Y12 with recent stent | **Delay elective surgery: ≥6 months after DES** (≥3 months if surgery delay is harmful); 12 months after ACS ideally. Keep aspirin. |
=== Anticoagulants (PAUSE / BRIDGE)
| Drug | Low/moderate bleed risk | High bleed risk |
|---|---|---|
| Apixaban / rivaroxaban / edoxaban | Hold **1 day** | Hold **2 days** |
| Dabigatran (CrCl ≥50) | 1 day | 2 days |
| Dabigatran (CrCl <50) | 2 days | 4 days |
| Warfarin | Hold **5 days**, INR day before | Same |

- **No bridging** for AF (BRIDGE). Bridge only selected: mechanical **mitral** valve, recent (<3 months) VTE/stroke, very high thrombotic risk.
- Restart DOAC 24 h after low-risk, 48–72 h after high-risk surgery.
=== Diabetes & others
- Basal insulin: give 75–80% night before / morning of surgery.
- GLP-1 RA: most continue; for high-aspiration-risk patients, liquid diet 24 h before (2024 multisociety guidance).
- Stress-dose steroids if chronic steroid user (see adrenal page).
:::

## ✍️ How to write the note

> "Patient is at **elevated** risk (RCRI 2: insulin-treated DM, Cr 2.3) for **intermediate-risk** surgery. Functional capacity ≥4 METs. No active cardiac conditions. No further testing will change management. Recommend: continue metoprolol and atorvastatin, hold empagliflozin 3 days prior, post-op troponin ×2 given elevated BNP."

## 🎓 Board corner

| Stem | Answer |
|---|---|
| Elevated risk, climbs 3 flights | Proceed without testing |
| Stable CAD, abnormal stress test, elective hip | Revascularization does not reduce periop risk — proceed with medical therapy |
| DES 2 months ago, elective cholecystectomy | Delay if possible (≥6 months) |
| AF on apixaban, low-bleed surgery | Hold 1 day, no bridge |
| Start metoprolol morning of surgery? | No (POISE: stroke, death) |
