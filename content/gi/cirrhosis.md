:::bottom
- **Every admitted cirrhotic with ascites → diagnostic paracentesis** (even if "just here for HE").
- **SBP = ascitic PMN ≥250** → **ceftriaxone 2 g/day** + **albumin 1.5 g/kg day 1, 1 g/kg day 3**.
- **HE:** find the trigger (infection, bleed, constipation, lytes, sedatives) + **lactulose** to 2–3 soft BMs/day + **rifaximin**.
- **AKI:** stop diuretics/NSAIDs, **albumin 1 g/kg × 2 days**. No response → **HRS-AKI → terlipressin (or norepinephrine) + albumin**.
- **MELD ≥15 or any decompensation → transplant referral.**
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Scar → high resistance → **portal hypertension**.
- Splanchnic **vasodilation** (NO) → low effective arterial volume → **RAAS/ADH/sympathetic** activation → salt and water retention → **ascites, hyponatremia**.
- Same "underfilling" → **kidney vasoconstriction → HRS-AKI**.
- Gut bacteria translocate → **SBP**. Ammonia and other toxins bypass the liver → **HE**.

## 🔎 Diagnosis

| Test | Interpretation |
|---|---|
| **SAAG** (serum − ascites albumin) | **≥1.1 → portal hypertension** (cirrhosis, HF, Budd-Chiari) · <1.1 → peritoneal (cancer, TB, pancreatic, nephrotic) |
| Ascitic total protein | <1.5 g/dL → high SBP risk; >2.5 + SAAG ≥1.1 → cardiac ascites |
| Ascitic PMN | **≥250 = SBP** |
| Culture | **Bedside blood-culture bottles** (10 mL each) |
| Secondary peritonitis clue | 2 of: protein >1, glucose <50, LDH > serum ULN; or polymicrobial → CT, surgery |

**Scores:** **MELD 3.0** (bilirubin, INR, Cr, Na, albumin, sex) · Child-Pugh A/B/C.

**HE grades (West Haven):** 1 subtle · 2 lethargy, asterixis · 3 somnolent, confused · 4 coma. Ammonia level does **not** diagnose or guide therapy.

## 💊 Management

:::tabs
=== Ascites
- Na **2 g/day**. Fluid restriction only if Na <125.
- **Spironolactone 100 mg + furosemide 40 mg** (ratio 100:40), up to 400:160.
- **Large-volume paracentesis** for tense ascites; **albumin 6–8 g per liter removed if >5 L**.
- Refractory → serial LVP or **TIPS**.
- Avoid ACEi/ARB, NSAIDs.
=== SBP
- Ceftriaxone 2 g IV daily (or cefotaxime) × 5–7 days. Nosocomial / recent abx → broader (pip-tazo, meropenem).
- **Albumin 1.5 g/kg day 1 + 1 g/kg day 3** (esp. Cr >1, BUN >30, bilirubin >4).
- **Secondary prophylaxis** after SBP: ciprofloxacin 500 mg daily (or TMP-SMX).
=== HE
- Treat precipitants. Hold sedatives/opioids.
- **Lactulose 25 mL q1–2 h until BM, then titrate to 2–3 soft BMs/day.** PEG 3350 is an alternative.
- **Rifaximin 550 mg BID** (add for recurrence).
- Protein **do not restrict** (1.2–1.5 g/kg/day); late-evening snack.
=== HRS-AKI
**Criteria:** cirrhosis + ascites; Cr ↑ ≥0.3 in 48 h or ≥50%; no response after 2 days of diuretic stop + albumin 1 g/kg; no shock; no nephrotoxins; no structural kidney disease (protein <500 mg/d, <50 RBC/hpf, normal US).
- **Terlipressin** 1 mg IV q4–6h (or infusion) + albumin. Avoid if SpO₂ <90%, volume overloaded, ACLF grade 3 (respiratory failure risk).
- **Norepinephrine** + albumin (ICU) if terlipressin not an option.
- Liver transplant is the definitive treatment.
:::

## 🏠 Admission checklist

- [ ] Diagnostic paracentesis (cell count, albumin, protein, cultures)
- [ ] Triggers: infection, GI bleed, alcohol, drugs, HCC, portal vein thrombosis (Doppler)
- [ ] Hold NSAIDs, ACEi/ARB; review sedatives, PPI need
- [ ] VTE prophylaxis (INR does not protect)
- [ ] Nutrition consult; thiamine/folate if alcohol
- [ ] Alcohol use disorder treatment (acamprosate, baclofen; avoid naltrexone in decompensation)
- [ ] Vaccines, HCC screen, variceal plan (carvedilol), transplant referral

## 🎓 Board corner

| Stem | Answer |
|---|---|
| Ascitic PMN 380, abdominal pain | SBP → ceftriaxone + albumin |
| SAAG 1.4, protein 3.0 | Cardiac ascites (or Budd-Chiari) |
| Confused cirrhotic, asterixis, constipated | HE → lactulose; find trigger |
| Cr 2.4 not improving after albumin, no shock | HRS-AKI → terlipressin + albumin |
| Polymicrobial ascites, protein 2.5, glucose 30 | Secondary peritonitis → CT, surgery |
