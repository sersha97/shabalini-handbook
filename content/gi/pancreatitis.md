:::bottom
- **Diagnosis = 2 of 3:** typical epigastric pain · **lipase >3× ULN** · imaging findings. No CT needed if the first two are present.
- **Moderate fluids:** LR ~1.5 mL/kg/h (bolus 10 mL/kg only if hypovolemic). **Aggressive fluids cause harm** (WATERFALL).
- **Feed early** (low-fat oral diet as tolerated, within 24–48 h). Tube feeds (NG/NJ) if cannot eat — not TPN.
- **No prophylactic antibiotics.** Antibiotics only for proven infection (cholangitis, infected necrosis).
- **Gallstone pancreatitis:** cholecystectomy **same admission** (mild). **ERCP within 24 h only if cholangitis**.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Trypsinogen activates **inside** the pancreas → autodigestion → local inflammation → cytokine storm → **SIRS, capillary leak, organ failure**.
- Third-spacing → hemoconcentration, AKI, hypocalcemia (fat saponification).
- Severity is driven by **persistent organ failure (>48 h)** and infected necrosis.

## 🔎 Diagnosis & cause

| Cause | Clue / test |
|---|---|
| **Gallstones** (~40%) | ALT >150 (3× ULN), RUQ ultrasound for all |
| **Alcohol** (~30%) | History; usually after years of heavy use |
| Hypertriglyceridemia | **TG >1000** |
| Hypercalcemia | Ca |
| Post-ERCP | Recent procedure |
| Drugs | Azathioprine, valproate, didanosine, GLP-1 RA (?), thiazides, estrogens, SGLT2i (with DKA) |
| Others | Tumor (age >40, first episode → imaging), autoimmune (IgG4), trauma, scorpion |

| Revised Atlanta | |
|---|---|
| **Mild** | No organ failure, no local complications |
| **Moderately severe** | Transient organ failure (<48 h) or local complications |
| **Severe** | **Persistent organ failure >48 h** |

- **CT with contrast** only if diagnosis unclear or no improvement after **48–72 h** (necrosis takes time to show).
- Risk markers: BUN rising, Hct >44, SIRS, age >60, obesity, BISAP ≥3.

## 💊 Management

:::tabs
=== First 72 hours
- **LR** 1.5 mL/kg/h, reassess every 6–12 h (UOP, HR, BUN, Hct). Stop IV fluids when eating.
- **Pain:** opioids are fine (hydromorphone, fentanyl). Multimodal.
- **Diet:** start low-fat solid or soft diet when hungry/pain improving — no need to wait for lipase to normalize.
- VTE prophylaxis. Glucose control.
=== Gallstones
- **Cholangitis** (fever, jaundice, rising bilirubin) → ERCP ≤24 h + antibiotics.
- Mild biliary pancreatitis → **cholecystectomy before discharge**.
- Severe / necrotizing → delay surgery until collections settle (~6 weeks).
=== Hypertriglyceridemia
- Insulin infusion (with dextrose if glucose normal) lowers TG; plasmapheresis in selected severe cases.
- Long-term: fibrate, omega-3, diet, glucose control, avoid alcohol.
=== Necrosis
- Sterile necrosis → supportive.
- **Infected necrosis** (gas on CT, clinical deterioration after 7–10 days) → **carbapenem** or quinolone + metronidazole; **step-up approach**: antibiotics → percutaneous/endoscopic drainage → necrosectomy, ideally after walled-off (≥4 weeks).
:::

## ⚠️ Pitfalls

- 250 mL/h "for 48 hours" → fluid overload.
- Making the patient NPO for days.
- Meropenem "just in case."
- Discharging gallstone pancreatitis without cholecystectomy → 30% recurrence.
