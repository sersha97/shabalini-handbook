:::bottom
- **Definition:** temp ≥38.3 °C once (or ≥38.0 °C for 1 h) **+ ANC <500** (or <1000 expected to fall <500).
- **Antibiotics within 60 minutes:** **cefepime 2 g q8h** (or pip-tazo / meropenem). Draw **2 sets of blood cultures** first (one from each lumen of a line).
- **Add vancomycin only for:** shock, catheter infection, skin/soft-tissue infection, pneumonia, MRSA colonization, gram-positives in blood.
- **Signs of infection may be absent** — no neutrophils = no pus, no infiltrate, no pyuria.
- **MASCC ≥21 = low risk** → may qualify for oral therapy/outpatient (with oncology).
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Chemotherapy kills fast-dividing cells: **neutrophils** (nadir day 7–14) and **gut lining** (mucositis) → gut bacteria translocate.
- Gram-negative rods (*Pseudomonas*, *E. coli*, *Klebsiella*) kill fastest → the empiric drug **must cover Pseudomonas**.
- Longer neutropenia (>7 days) → fungi (*Candida*, *Aspergillus*).

## 🔎 Diagnosis & workup

- Full exam: **mouth, skin, line sites, perianal area (look, no rectal exam)**, lungs, sinuses.
- Labs: CBC with diff, CMP, lactate, **blood cultures ×2**, UA + urine culture, CXR, respiratory viral PCR as indicated, C. diff if diarrhea.
- CT chest if respiratory symptoms (CXR is often normal); CT sinuses if symptoms.

|---|---|
| Burden of illness: none/mild | 5 |
| No hypotension | 5 |
| No COPD | 4 |
| Solid tumor or no prior fungal infection | 4 |

## 💊 Management

:::tabs
=== Empiric
| Situation | Regimen |
|---|---|
| Standard | **Cefepime 2 g IV q8h** · or pip-tazo 4.5 g q6h · or meropenem 1 g q8h |
| Penicillin allergy (severe) | Aztreonam + vancomycin (± aminoglycoside) |
| Add **vancomycin** | Shock, suspected line infection, SSTI, pneumonia, MRSA colonization, GPC in blood |
| Abdominal source / C. diff risk | Pip-tazo or meropenem (+ oral vanc if C. diff) |
| Low risk, outpatient (MASCC ≥21) | Ciprofloxacin + amoxicillin-clavulanate (if no FQ prophylaxis) |
=== Persistent fever
- Fever 3–5 days is common — **do not change antibiotics just for fever** if stable.
- **Fever >4–7 days with expected long neutropenia** → CT chest, **galactomannan/β-D-glucan**, start empiric **antifungal** (voriconazole/echinocandin/liposomal amphotericin).
- Clinical worsening → broaden (meropenem, + vanc, + antifungal).
=== Duration
- Unexplained fever: continue until afebrile ≥48 h and ANC recovering (>500) — or stop earlier if clinically stable and afebrile ≥72 h (ECIL-4 approach, with expert input).
- Documented infection: treat that infection's standard course.
- **G-CSF** not routine for established febrile neutropenia (consider in high-risk sepsis).
:::

## ⚠️ Pitfalls

- Waiting for the CBC result before antibiotics in a febrile chemo patient.
- Rectal temperatures/exams (bacteremia risk).
- Reflex vancomycin for every case.
- Forgetting antifungal coverage after a week of fever.
