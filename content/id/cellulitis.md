:::bottom
- **Non-purulent cellulitis = strep** → **cefazolin IV / cephalexin PO**. No routine MRSA coverage.
- **Purulent (abscess) = staph** → **incision & drainage** is the main treatment; add MRSA coverage (TMP-SMX, doxycycline; vancomycin IV) if systemic signs or large/complex.
- **Elevate** the leg. Treat tinea and edema to stop recurrence.
- **Bilateral "cellulitis" is almost never cellulitis** — think stasis dermatitis.
- **Pain out of proportion, rapid spread, bullae, crepitus, shock** → **necrotizing infection → surgery now** + vanc + pip-tazo + clindamycin.
- **Duration: 5–6 days** if improving.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Break in skin (tinea between toes, wound, edema cracks) → bacteria into **dermis + subcutaneous fat**.
- **Erysipelas** = superficial dermis, raised sharp border (strep).
- **Necrotizing fasciitis** = infection tracks along the fascia, thromboses vessels → skin looks better than the tissue below. Type I polymicrobial (diabetes, perineum = Fournier), type II *S. pyogenes* ± *S. aureus*.

## 🔎 Diagnosis

| Mimic | Clue |
|---|---|
| **Stasis dermatitis** | Bilateral, itchy, chronic, no fever |
| DVT | Calf swelling — Doppler |
| Gout / pseudogout | Joint centered, crystals |
| Contact dermatitis | Itch, sharp edges, vesicles |
| Lipodermatosclerosis | "Inverted champagne bottle" leg |
| Erythema migrans | Expanding ring, tick exposure |

- Mark the border with a pen and date. Some redness **spreads in the first 24–48 h** even on the right antibiotic.
- Blood cultures only with systemic toxicity, immunosuppression, animal bite, immersion injury.
- **POCUS** finds hidden abscesses (cobblestoning vs fluid pocket).

## 💊 Management

:::tabs
=== Non-purulent
| Severity | Regimen |
|---|---|
| Mild (PO) | Cephalexin 500 mg QID · or amoxicillin · (PCN allergy: clindamycin) |
| Moderate (IV) | **Cefazolin 2 g q8h** |
| Severe / sepsis | Vancomycin + pip-tazo (search for necrotizing infection) |

5 days; extend if not improved.
=== Purulent / abscess
- **I&D** (+ packing optional). Send culture.
- Add antibiotic if: abscess >2 cm, multiple sites, cellulitis around it, systemic signs, immunosuppression, extremes of age.
- **TMP-SMX DS 1–2 tab BID** or **doxycycline 100 mg BID** (5–7 days). IV: **vancomycin**.
=== Necrotizing infection
- **Surgical debridement** — do not wait for imaging if suspicion is high (CT gas is late; LRINEC is unreliable).
- **Vancomycin + pip-tazo (or meropenem) + clindamycin** (toxin suppression).
- *Vibrio* (sea water, cirrhosis) → doxycycline + ceftazidime.
=== Diabetic foot
- Probe-to-bone positive or ESR >70 → **osteomyelitis** → MRI, bone biopsy.
- Mild: cephalexin / amox-clav. Moderate–severe: amp-sulbactam, ceftriaxone + metronidazole, or pip-tazo ± vanc (MRSA risk).
- Off-loading, vascular study (ABI), podiatry, glucose control.
:::

## ⚠️ Pitfalls

- Admitting bilateral stasis dermatitis for IV vancomycin.
- Antibiotics without draining the abscess.
- Missing necrotizing fasciitis because "the skin looks OK."
- 14-day courses.

## 🎓 Board corner

| Stem | Answer |
|---|---|
| Unilateral warm red leg, no pus, afebrile | Cephalexin |
| Fluctuant 4 cm abscess, fever | I&D + TMP-SMX or doxycycline |
| Severe pain, bullae, hypotension, crepitus | Necrotizing fasciitis → OR |
| Cirrhosis, oyster/salt water wound, bullae, shock | *Vibrio vulnificus* |
| Cat bite on hand | Amoxicillin-clavulanate (*Pasteurella*) |
