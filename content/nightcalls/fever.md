:::bottom
- **First question: is the patient sick?** Vitals, mental status, lactate, NEWS2. Sick → **sepsis pathway** (cultures + antibiotics ≤1 h).
- Well-appearing, stable → **look for the source before antibiotics**: lungs, urine, lines, wounds, C. diff, legs (DVT), drugs, transfusion.
- **Blood cultures ×2** (peripheral + line) before any antibiotic.
- Do not reflexively order a UA/urine culture without urinary symptoms (bacteriuria is common and misleading).
- **Neutropenic? → antibiotics now** (see neutropenic fever).
:::

## 📞 Phone questions

- Temp, HR, BP, RR, SpO₂, mental status — trend?
- Why admitted? Immunosuppressed? Neutropenic? Recent surgery/procedure?
- Lines, catheters, drains, wounds? New meds/transfusion?
- **Orders:** blood cultures ×2, CBC with diff, BMP, lactate, CXR if respiratory, UA **only if urinary symptoms/catheter + systemic signs**, acetaminophen for comfort.

## 🔎 Sources — think through the body

| Source | Clue |
|---|---|
| Lungs | Cough, O₂ need ↑, aspiration risk |
| Lines | Erythema, purulence at site; **central line >48 h** |
| Urine | Catheter, flank pain, retention |
| Skin / wounds | Surgical site, pressure ulcers |
| C. difficile | Diarrhea ≥3/24 h + antibiotics |
| **DVT / PE** | Leg swelling, hypoxia |
| Drug fever | β-lactams, anticonvulsants, sulfa — eosinophilia, rash |
| Transfusion | During/within hours of blood |
| Others | Gout, pancreatitis, acalculous cholecystitis, alcohol withdrawal, thyroid, adrenal |

:::pearl Post-op fever — "6 W's"
**Wind** (atelectasis is *not* a proven cause — look for pneumonia, day 1–3) · **Water** (UTI, day 3–5) · **Walking** (DVT/PE, day 5+) · **Wound** (day 5–7) · **Wonder drugs** (any time) · **What did we do** (lines, procedures). Fever in first 24–48 h after surgery is usually inflammatory — examine, don't reflexively culture.
:::

## 💊 Management

:::flow
Q: Septic (hypotension, lactate >2, AMS, new organ dysfunction) or neutropenic?
Y: Cultures → broad antibiotics ≤1 h → fluids → call senior/RRT.
Q: Clear source on exam?
Y: Treat it (source-directed antibiotics, remove line, drain).
N: Cultures, CXR, observe; acetaminophen; reassess in 2–4 h. No antibiotics if stable and no source.
:::

## ⚠️ Pitfalls

- Pan-culture + vanc/pip-tazo for every 38.1 °C.
- Treating asymptomatic bacteriuria found on a reflex UA.
- Missing **C. diff** or a **DVT**.
- Forgetting to look under dressings and at line sites.

## 🎓 Board corner

| Stem | Answer |
|---|---|
| POD 1, T 38.2, well, clear lungs | Observation (inflammatory), incentive spirometry |
| Fever + rash + eosinophilia on day 10 of β-lactam | Drug fever / DRESS → stop drug |
| Central line day 8, fever, no other source | Line infection → cultures, remove line |
| High fever + rigidity on haloperidol | NMS → stop drug, cool, dantrolene/bromocriptine |
