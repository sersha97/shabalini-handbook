:::bottom
- **On the phone:** repeat **manual BP** both arms, HR, SpO₂, mental status, UOP. Lay flat / legs up. **Go see the patient.**
- **Shock = hypoperfusion** (confused, cold/mottled, low UOP, lactate >2) — not a number.
- **4 types:** hypovolemic · distributive (sepsis) · cardiogenic · obstructive (PE, tamponade, tension PTX). **POCUS** sorts them in minutes.
- **Fluid challenge 500 mL** balanced crystalloid unless overloaded/cardiogenic. Reassess.
- **No response or MAP <65 → norepinephrine + call RRT/ICU.**
:::

## 📞 Phone questions

- Real? (cuff size, arm, manual repeat)
- **Trend** — baseline BP? (SBP 95 may be normal for her)
- Symptoms: confusion, chest pain, dyspnea, bleeding, melena?
- New meds in last 6 h: **antihypertensives, opioids, sedatives, diuretics, nitro**, dialysis today?
- **Orders:** repeat vitals, 12-lead ECG, POC glucose, lactate, CBC, BMP, cultures if febrile; IV access ×2; hold antihypertensives.

## 👀 Bedside in 60 seconds

| Look | Tells you |
|---|---|
| Mental status | Brain perfusion |
| Skin: warm vs cold, mottling, **cap refill >3 s** | Distributive (warm) vs low-output (cold) |
| JVP | Low = hypovolemic/distributive · High = cardiogenic/obstructive |
| Lungs | Crackles (HF), unilateral absent sounds (PTX) |
| Abdomen / stool / drains | Bleeding |
| Lines, wounds | Source of sepsis |

## 🔊 POCUS — "pump, tank, pipes"

| Type | Heart (pump) | IVC (tank) | Lungs | Other |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Hypovolemic** | Hyperdynamic, small | Small, collapsing | A-lines | Free fluid (bleed) |
| **Distributive** | Hyperdynamic (or septic CM) | Small/normal | Normal or consolidation | Source |
| **Cardiogenic** | **Poor squeeze** | Plethoric | **B-lines** | |
| **Obstructive** | Effusion + RV collapse (**tamponade**) / **dilated RV** (PE) | Plethoric | No sliding (**PTX**) | DVT on leg |

## 💊 Management

:::flow
Q: Shock signs (AMS, mottled, lactate >2, low UOP)?
Y: Call RRT. Two IVs. 500 mL bolus (if not cardiogenic). Norepinephrine early (peripheral OK).
N: Likely meds/benign → hold BP meds, recheck in 30–60 min.
Q: Which type on exam + POCUS?
Y: Bleed → transfuse, reverse anticoagulation, GI/surgery · Sepsis → cultures + antibiotics ≤1 h · Cardiogenic → see shock page · Tamponade → pericardiocentesis · Tension PTX → needle decompression · PE → heparin ± lysis
Q: Not better after 1–2 L or needs pressor?
Y: ICU transfer.
:::

:::pearl
Also think: **adrenal crisis** (long-term steroids), **anaphylaxis** (new antibiotic/contrast → epinephrine IM 0.5 mg), **autonomic/neurogenic**, **post-dialysis**, arrhythmia (check ECG).
:::

## ⚠️ Pitfalls

- Treating the monitor's cuff reading without a manual check.
- 3 L of fluid to a patient with an EF of 15%.
- Waiting for "one more bolus" instead of starting pressor.
- Forgetting the evening dose of carvedilol + amlodipine + hydralazine all given at 21:00.
