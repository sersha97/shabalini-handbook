:::bottom
- **Mechanism first:** V/Q mismatch, shunt, hypoventilation, diffusion, low FiO₂. Use the **A–a gradient**.
- **HFNC** is first-line for most hypoxemic failure (not hypercapnic). Start 40–60 L/min.
- **BiPAP** for **COPD with hypercapnia** and **cardiogenic pulmonary edema**.
- **ROX index** <3.85 at 2–12 h → HFNC failing → **intubate early**, do not wait for arrest.
- **ARDS:** low tidal volume **6 mL/kg PBW**, plateau <30, PEEP, **prone if P/F <150**.
- Target **SpO₂ 92–96%** (88–92% in CO₂ retainers).
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

| Mechanism | A–a gradient | O₂ fixes it? | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| **V/Q mismatch** | High | Yes | Pneumonia, COPD, PE |
| **Shunt** | High | **No** (poorly) | ARDS, lobar collapse, intracardiac shunt |
| Hypoventilation | Normal | Yes | Opioids, neuromuscular, obesity |
| Diffusion limit | High | Yes | ILD (worse with exercise) |
| Low FiO₂ | Normal | Yes | Altitude |

A–a gradient = [FiO₂ × 713 − PaCO₂/0.8] − PaO₂. Normal ≈ age/4 + 4.

## 🔎 Diagnosis

- ABG (PaO₂, PaCO₂), CXR, **POCUS** (B-lines, consolidation, effusion, pneumothorax, RV), ECG, BNP, procalcitonin/cultures, CTPA if PE likely.

| ARDS (2023 global definition) | |
|---|---|
| Timing | Within 1 week of a known insult |
| Imaging | Bilateral opacities not fully explained by effusion, collapse, nodules |
| Origin | Not fully explained by HF or volume overload |
| Oxygenation | P/F ≤300 or **SpO₂/FiO₂ ≤315** (SpO₂ ≤97%) on PEEP ≥5 **or HFNC ≥30 L/min** |
| Severity (intubated) | Mild 200–300 · Moderate 100–200 · Severe ≤100 |

## 💊 Management

:::tabs
=== Escalation
| Device | Start | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Nasal cannula | 1–6 L | FiO₂ ~24–44% |
| Venturi | 24–60% | Precise FiO₂ — good for COPD |
| **HFNC** | 40–60 L/min, FiO₂ to target | Washes out dead space, little PEEP. FLORALI |
| **BiPAP** | IPAP 10–12 / EPAP 5 | Hypercapnia, cardiogenic edema |
| Intubation | | Rising work of breathing, AMS, shock, ROX failing, cannot protect airway |

**ROX = (SpO₂/FiO₂) ÷ RR.** ≥4.88 at 2, 6, 12 h → likely success. <3.85 → likely failure.
=== Lung-protective ventilation
- **Vt 6 mL/kg PBW** (4–8). PBW men = 50 + 2.3 × (height in − 60); women = 45.5 + 2.3 × (in − 60).
- **Plateau <30**, **driving pressure <15**.
- PEEP by PEEP/FiO₂ table; higher PEEP in moderate–severe ARDS.
- **Prone ≥12–16 h/day** if P/F <150 (PROSEVA).
- Neuromuscular blockade only if dyssynchrony despite sedation.
- **Conservative fluids** once shock resolved (FACTT).
- Corticosteroids: suggested in ARDS (2024 ATS, conditional).
- Refractory → VV-ECMO center.
=== Sudden desaturation on the vent (DOPE)
**D**isplacement · **O**bstruction (plug, kink) · **P**neumothorax · **E**quipment. Plus breath stacking → disconnect briefly. Bag with 100% O₂ while you check.
:::

## ⚠️ Pitfalls

- HFNC "working" while work of breathing climbs — delayed intubation raises mortality.
- Using actual body weight for tidal volume.
- BiPAP in a patient who cannot protect the airway or is vomiting.
- Hyperoxia (SpO₂ 100% for days).

## 🎓 Board corner

| Stem | Answer |
|---|---|
| PaO₂ low, PaCO₂ high, normal A–a | Hypoventilation (opioids, NM disease) |
| Hypoxemia not improving with 100% O₂ | Shunt |
| ARDS, P/F 110 on PEEP 12 | Prone positioning |
| Hypoxemic pneumonia, no hypercapnia | HFNC first |
| Plateau 34 on Vt 8 mL/kg | Lower tidal volume to 6 |
