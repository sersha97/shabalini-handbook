:::bottom
- **Albuterol** (continuous 10–15 mg/h if severe) + **ipratropium** first hours + **steroids within 1 hour**.
- **Severe or not responding → magnesium 2 g IV** over 20 min.
- **Danger signs:** cannot speak, silent chest, drowsy, **normal or rising PaCO₂** (tiring out). → ICU.
- O₂ target **93–95%**.
- Intubation is high-risk: ketamine, big tube, **low rate, long expiration**.
- Discharge: **ICS-containing controller** (ICS-formoterol MART) — **never SABA alone**.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Trigger (virus, allergen, smoke, NSAID, cold air) → **bronchospasm + airway edema + mucus plugs**.
- Air trapping → auto-PEEP → high work of breathing and low venous return (hypotension, pulsus).
- Early: hyperventilation → **low PaCO₂**. A "normal" PaCO₂ (40) in a struggling asthmatic = **fatigue** → impending failure.

## 🔎 Severity

| Feature | Moderate | Severe | Life-threatening |
|---|---|---|---|
| Speech | Phrases | Words | Cannot speak, drowsy |
| RR | <30 | >30 | Bradypnea |
| Accessory muscles | Some | Yes | Paradoxical breathing |
| PEF (% predicted) | >50% | ≤50% | <25% or cannot do |
| SpO₂ | 90–95% | <90% | Cyanosis |
| PaCO₂ | Low | Low/normal | **Normal → high** |

Workup: PEF, VBG/ABG if severe, CXR only if pneumothorax/pneumonia suspected or no response. Check potassium (albuterol lowers K) and lactate (albuterol raises it).

## 💊 Management

| Drug | Dose |
|---|---|
| **Albuterol** | 2.5–5 mg neb q20 min ×3, or **continuous 10–15 mg/h**; or MDI 4–8 puffs q20 min with spacer |
| **Ipratropium** | 0.5 mg neb q20 min ×3 (first hours only) |
| **Prednisone** | 40–50 mg PO daily × 5–7 days (or methylprednisolone 40–80 mg IV if cannot take PO) |
| **Magnesium sulfate** | 2 g IV over 20 min (severe / poor response) |
| Epinephrine | 0.3–0.5 mg IM if anaphylaxis or near-arrest |
| Ketamine | Bronchodilator; induction agent of choice |

:::tabs
=== NIV / intubation
- BiPAP: limited evidence, short trial only in an alert, cooperative patient.
- **Intubation:** ketamine 1–2 mg/kg + rocuronium; ETT ≥8.0.
- **Vent:** rate 10–12, Vt 6–8 mL/kg, high inspiratory flow (80–100 L/min), I:E ≥1:4. Accept PaCO₂ high (pH >7.15–7.20).
- Hypotension after intubation → **disconnect** from vent, press on chest → let air out (auto-PEEP). Rule out pneumothorax.
=== Discharge
- [ ] PEF >60–70% and stable 4–6 h after last bronchodilator
- [ ] Steroid burst 5–7 days (no taper)
- [ ] **ICS-formoterol** as maintenance + reliever (GINA Track 1)
- [ ] Inhaler technique; written action plan; trigger avoidance
- [ ] Follow-up 2–7 days; consider biologic referral (frequent severe exacerbations)
:::

## ⚠️ Pitfalls

- Reassured by a "normal" PaCO₂.
- Sedating an anxious asthmatic outside the ICU.
- Theophylline / IV aminophylline (little benefit, toxic).
- Sending home on albuterol alone.

## 🎓 Board corner

| Stem | Answer |
|---|---|
| Asthma attack, PaCO₂ 44, drowsy | Impending respiratory failure → ICU / intubate |
| No response to 3 nebs + steroids | IV magnesium |
| Asthma + nasal polyps + aspirin reaction | AERD (Samter) |
| Mild intermittent asthma, adult — reliever? | As-needed low-dose ICS-formoterol |
| Intubated asthmatic, BP drops | Auto-PEEP → disconnect; check for pneumothorax |
