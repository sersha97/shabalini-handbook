:::bottom
- **SpO₂ target 88–92%.** Too much O₂ → more CO₂ retention.
- **Albuterol + ipratropium** nebs, **prednisone 40 mg × 5 days**.
- **Antibiotics** if more **sputum purulence** + more dyspnea or volume, or if on NIV/ventilator.
- **BiPAP** if **pH <7.35 and PaCO₂ >45** — it saves lives and avoids intubation.
- Rule out the mimics: **PE, HF, pneumonia, pneumothorax**, ACS.
- Discharge: long-acting inhalers, **pulmonary rehab within 4 weeks**, smoking cessation, vaccines.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Trigger (virus ~50%, bacteria, pollution) → more airway inflammation, mucus and bronchospasm.
- **Air trapping** (dynamic hyperinflation): the patient cannot breathe out fully before the next breath → flat diaphragm, high work of breathing → fatigue → CO₂ rises.
- O₂ excess worsens hypercapnia by **V/Q mismatch** (loss of hypoxic vasoconstriction) and the **Haldane effect** — not mainly "loss of drive."

## 🔎 Diagnosis

- **Exacerbation (GOLD/Rome):** dyspnea and/or cough-sputum worse within **≤14 days**.
- Severity (Rome): mild / moderate / severe by RR, HR, SpO₂, CRP, and ABG (PaCO₂, pH).
- Workup: **VBG/ABG**, CXR, ECG, CBC (eosinophils), BMP, BNP/troponin if cardiac doubt, viral PCR; **CTPA if PE suspected** (PE is found in ~1 in 6 unexplained exacerbations).

## 💊 Management

:::flow
Q: Hypercapnic acidosis (pH <7.35, PaCO₂ >45)?
Y: BiPAP: IPAP 10–12 / EPAP 5; ABG in 1–2 h. Worsening, can't protect airway → intubate.
N: O₂ to 88–92% (Venturi mask or nasal cannula).
Q: Bronchodilators
Y: Albuterol 2.5 mg + ipratropium 0.5 mg neb q4–6 h (albuterol q1–2 h as needed early).
Q: Steroids
Y: Prednisone 40 mg PO daily × 5 days (IV only if cannot take PO).
Q: Antibiotics?
Y: Purulent sputum + 1 other cardinal symptom, or on NIV/MV → 5 days.
:::

| Antibiotic choice | When |
|---|---|
| Azithromycin 500 mg day 1 → 250 mg × 4 d | Uncomplicated |
| Doxycycline 100 mg BID | Uncomplicated |
| Amoxicillin-clavulanate 875 mg BID | Older, frequent exacerbations, FEV₁ <50% |
| Levofloxacin 750 mg / cefepime / pip-tazo | **Pseudomonas risk** (bronchiectasis, prior isolate, frequent abx) |

:::pearl
**Eosinophils ≥300** predict steroid and ICS response. Low eosinophils → lower steroid benefit.
:::

## 🏠 Discharge bundle

- [ ] Inhaler technique checked; **LAMA + LABA**; add **ICS** if eosinophils ≥300, or ≥100 with frequent exacerbations (avoid ICS with recurrent pneumonia)
- [ ] Biologic referral (dupilumab/mepolizumab) if eos ≥300 and exacerbations despite triple therapy
- [ ] **Pulmonary rehab** within 4 weeks
- [ ] Smoking cessation (varenicline/NRT); vaccines (influenza, COVID, pneumococcal, RSV ≥60, Tdap)
- [ ] Home O₂ evaluation (resting SpO₂ ≤88% or PaO₂ ≤55)
- [ ] Follow-up within 1–4 weeks; action plan

## ⚠️ Pitfalls

- 100% non-rebreather in a CO₂ retainer.
- Missing **PE** or **HF** as the real cause.
- 14-day steroid tapers (no benefit over 5 days).
- BiPAP delaying needed intubation.

## 🎓 Board corner

| Stem | Answer |
|---|---|
| pH 7.28, PaCO₂ 68, alert | BiPAP |
| pH 7.15, drowsy, vomiting | Intubate |
| Exacerbation, purulent sputum, more dyspnea | Antibiotics + steroids |
| Best long-term survival interventions | Smoking cessation, O₂ (if hypoxemic), (LVR/transplant selected) |
| Steroid duration | 5 days (REDUCE) |
