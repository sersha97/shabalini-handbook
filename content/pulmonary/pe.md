:::bottom
- **Pretest probability first** (Wells / clinical gestalt). Low → **PERC** or **age-adjusted D-dimer** (age × 10 if >50).
- High probability → **CTPA** (start anticoagulation while you wait if bleed risk is low).
- **Risk-stratify:** shock? → **high risk → systemic lysis** (alteplase 100 mg / 2 h). RV strain + troponin → intermediate-high → monitor, rescue lysis if crash.
- **Most patients → DOAC** (apixaban 10 mg BID × 7 d → 5 mg BID). Low-risk (sPESI 0) → early discharge.
- Activate the **PERT** for intermediate-high and high risk.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- **Virchow:** stasis + endothelial injury + hypercoagulability → DVT (mostly proximal leg) → embolizes.
- Clot raises **pulmonary vascular resistance** → **RV pressure overload** → RV dilates, septum shifts left → LV underfilled → low output → shock.
- RV ischemia (high wall stress + low coronary flow) → troponin up → **death spiral**.
- Hypoxemia from **V/Q mismatch**; dead space → high minute ventilation, low PaCO₂.

:::analogy
The RV is a thin-walled sprinter, not a weightlifter. Ask it to push against a sudden clot and it fails fast. That is why RV size on echo predicts outcome.
:::

## 🔎 Diagnosis

| Wells criteria | Points |
|---|---|
| Clinical signs of DVT | 3 |
| PE most likely diagnosis | 3 |
| HR >100 | 1.5 |
| Immobilization ≥3 d or surgery <4 wk | 1.5 |
| Prior DVT/PE | 1.5 |
| Hemoptysis | 1 |
| Cancer (active) | 1 |

**≤4 = PE unlikely** → D-dimer. **>4 = likely** → CTPA.

- **PERC** (all 8 negative in low gestalt → no testing): age <50, HR <100, SpO₂ ≥95%, no hemoptysis, no estrogen, no prior VTE, no unilateral leg swelling, no recent surgery/trauma.
- **YEARS** algorithm: 0 items → D-dimer <1000 rules out; ≥1 → <500.
- CTPA contraindicated → **V/Q scan** (normal CXR) or leg Doppler (positive DVT = treat).
- ECG: sinus tachycardia (most common), S1Q3T3, RV strain, new RBBB.

## 🧪 Risk stratification

{{fig:pe-risk}}

| Tool | Low-risk if |
|---|---|
| **sPESI** | 0 points: age ≤80, no cancer, no chronic cardiopulmonary disease, HR <110, SBP ≥100, SpO₂ ≥90% |
| **Hestia** | All 11 criteria negative → outpatient |
| RV on CT/echo | RV/LV ratio <1.0 |
| Troponin / BNP | Normal |

## 💊 Management

:::tabs
=== Anticoagulation
| Drug | Dose |
|---|---|
| **Apixaban** | 10 mg BID × 7 d → 5 mg BID |
| **Rivaroxaban** | 15 mg BID × 21 d → 20 mg daily (with food) |
| Enoxaparin | 1 mg/kg SC q12h (→ DOAC or warfarin) |
| UFH | 80 U/kg bolus → 18 U/kg/h — use if lysis/procedure may be needed, CrCl <30, unstable |
| Dabigatran / edoxaban | After ≥5 d of parenteral therapy |

- Cancer-associated VTE: apixaban, rivaroxaban, edoxaban or LMWH (caution GI/GU cancers with bleeding).
- Avoid DOACs: antiphospholipid syndrome (triple positive) → warfarin; pregnancy → LMWH.
=== High risk / lysis
- **Systemic alteplase 100 mg IV over 2 h** (stop heparin during infusion, then restart).
- Cardiac arrest from PE → alteplase 50 mg bolus, continue CPR 60–90 min.
- Contraindicated / failed → **catheter-directed therapy** or surgical embolectomy; VA-ECMO bridge.
- Avoid big fluid boluses (RV is overloaded); **norepinephrine** for hypotension; avoid intubation if possible (positive pressure → collapse).
=== Duration
| Situation | Duration |
|---|---|
| Major transient provoker (surgery, trauma) | **3 months** |
| Minor/transient (travel, estrogen) | 3 months, consider longer |
| **Unprovoked** | Extended (indefinite) if bleeding risk acceptable; reduced-dose apixaban 2.5 mg BID after 6 mo is an option |
| Active cancer | While cancer active |
| Recurrent VTE | Indefinite |

- IVC filter **only** if anticoagulation is contraindicated (remove later).
- Subsegmental, single, no DVT, low risk → surveillance can be discussed.
:::

## ⚠️ Pitfalls

- D-dimer in high-probability patients (a negative does not rule out).
- Withholding anticoagulation while waiting hours for CT in high suspicion.
- Fluids for RV failure shock.
- Thrombophilia testing during the acute event (results unreliable on heparin/acute clot).

## 🎓 Board corner

| Stem | Answer |
|---|---|
| PE, BP 75/40 | Systemic thrombolysis |
| PE, normal BP, RV dilation + troponin up | Anticoagulate, monitor; rescue lysis if worsens |
| PE + active GI bleed | IVC filter |
| Pregnant, suspected PE | Leg Doppler / CTPA or V/Q; treat with LMWH |
| Unprovoked PE, done 6 months, low bleed risk | Continue (extended) anticoagulation |
| Triple-positive APS | Warfarin, not DOAC |
