:::bottom
- **Tap a new effusion** unless the cause is obvious (bilateral, HF responding to diuretics).
- **Light's criteria** separate exudate from transudate.
- Diuresed HF can look like an exudate → check **serum–fluid albumin gradient >1.2** or **NT-proBNP** in fluid.
- **Parapneumonic: pH <7.2, glucose <60, or pus/positive Gram stain → chest tube.**
- Use **ultrasound** for every tap.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

- Fluid enters the pleura from pleural capillaries and leaves via lymphatics.
- **Transudate** = pressure problem (high hydrostatic, low oncotic): HF, cirrhosis, nephrotic syndrome.
- **Exudate** = leaky membrane or blocked lymphatics: infection, cancer, PE, autoimmune, pancreatitis.

## 🔎 Diagnosis — Light's criteria

Exudate if **any one**:

| Criterion | Cut-off |
|---|---|
| Fluid protein ÷ serum protein | >0.5 |
| Fluid LDH ÷ serum LDH | >0.6 |
| Fluid LDH | >⅔ upper limit of normal serum LDH |

**Send:** protein, LDH, glucose, pH (in blood-gas syringe, on ice), cell count + differential, Gram stain + culture (in blood culture bottles), cytology (≥50–60 mL). Add as needed: triglycerides (chylothorax >110), amylase, adenosine deaminase (TB), hematocrit (hemothorax: fluid Hct >50% of blood).

| Clue | Think |
|---|---|
| Lymphocytic exudate | TB, cancer, lymphoma, post-CABG, RA |
| Glucose <60 | Empyema, RA, TB, cancer, lupus |
| pH <7.2 | Complicated parapneumonic/empyema, esophageal rupture |
| Milky | Chylothorax (TG >110) |
| Bloody | Cancer, PE, trauma |

## 💊 Management

:::tabs
=== Parapneumonic
| Type | Features | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Simple | pH ≥7.2, glucose ≥60, clear | Antibiotics only |
| **Complicated** | pH <7.2, glucose <60, loculated, + Gram stain | **Chest tube** + antibiotics |
| **Empyema** | Frank pus | Chest tube; **tPA + DNase** (MIST-2) if not draining; VATS if fails |

Antibiotics: cover anaerobes (e.g., ampicillin-sulbactam, or ceftriaxone + metronidazole). Usually 2–6 weeks.
=== Malignant
- Symptomatic → therapeutic tap.
- Recurrent → **indwelling pleural catheter** or pleurodesis (talc).
- Trapped lung → catheter, not pleurodesis.
=== Thoracentesis safety
- Ultrasound-guided; **INR up to ~2 and platelets >25–50 k are usually acceptable** (low bleed risk procedure).
- Stop at **1–1.5 L** or if chest pain/cough (re-expansion edema).
- Post-tap CXR only if symptoms or air aspirated.
:::

## ⚠️ Pitfalls

- Calling a diuresed HF effusion an exudate.
- No pH from the parapneumonic tap (or pH sent in a non-heparinized tube).
- Draining 3 L at once.

## 🎓 Board corner

| Stem | Answer |
|---|---|
| Fluid pH 7.0 after pneumonia | Complicated → chest tube |
| Lymphocytic exudate, high ADA | TB pleuritis |
| TG 300 after thoracic surgery | Chylothorax |
| Bilateral effusions, HF, exudate on Light's after diuresis | Check albumin gradient >1.2 → transudate |
