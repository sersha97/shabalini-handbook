:::bottom
- **KDIGO AKI:** Cr ↑ ≥0.3 in 48 h, or ≥1.5× baseline in 7 days, or UOP <0.5 mL/kg/h for 6 h.
- **Every AKI:** bladder scan, UA with **microscopy**, volume exam, med review, renal ultrasound if no clear cause.
- **Stop nephrotoxins** (NSAIDs, contrast if avoidable, aminoglycosides, vanc+pip-tazo combo), **hold ACEi/ARB/diuretics** if hypovolemic, **dose-adjust** everything.
- **Dialysis for AEIOU** emergencies — not for a creatinine number (STARRT-AKI).
- Do not withhold **needed** contrast imaging; risk is lower than once thought.
:::

## 🧠 Pathophysiology in 60 seconds

| Type | Mechanism | Common examples |
|---|---|---|
| **Prerenal** | Low renal perfusion; tubules intact | Hypovolemia, HF, cirrhosis (HRS), NSAIDs/ACEi |
| **Intrinsic** | Damage to tubules, interstitium, glomeruli, vessels | **ATN** (ischemia, sepsis, toxins), AIN, GN, TMA |
| **Postrenal** | Obstruction | BPH, retention (anticholinergics, opioids), stones, tumors |

- Prerenal → kidneys **hold sodium** → low urine Na, low FENa.
- ATN → tubules cannot hold sodium → **muddy brown casts**, FENa >2%.
- Creatinine **lags** behind injury by 24–48 h.

## 🔎 Diagnosis

| KDIGO stage | Creatinine | Urine output |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1.5–1.9× or ↑ ≥0.3 | <0.5 mL/kg/h × 6–12 h |
| 2 | 2.0–2.9× | <0.5 × ≥12 h |
| 3 | ≥3× or ≥4.0 or RRT | <0.3 × ≥24 h or anuria ≥12 h |

| Test | Prerenal | ATN |
|---|---|---|
| FENa | **<1%** | >2% |
| FEUrea (on diuretics) | **<35%** | >50% |
| BUN:Cr | >20 | 10–15 |
| Sediment | Bland / hyaline casts | **Muddy brown granular casts** |

| Sediment | Think |
|---|---|
| RBC casts, dysmorphic RBC | Glomerulonephritis → urgent serologies, nephrology |
| WBC casts, eosinophils, rash | AIN (PPI, β-lactams, NSAIDs) |
| Crystals | Acyclovir, methotrexate, ethylene glycol, uric acid |
| Heme + on dipstick, no RBC | Rhabdo / hemolysis |

## 💊 Management

:::flow
Q: Obstruction? (bladder scan >300 mL, hydronephrosis)
Y: Foley / urology / nephrostomy. Watch for post-obstructive diuresis.
Q: Hypovolemic?
Y: Balanced crystalloid bolus 500–1000 mL, reassess. Hold diuretics/ACEi/ARB.
N: Overloaded → diuretic trial; HF/cirrhosis-specific plans.
Q: Toxins or intrinsic signs?
Y: Stop offenders; send GN/AIN workup; nephrology.
Q: AEIOU emergency?
Y: Urgent dialysis.
:::

| AEIOU (dialysis indications) | |
|---|---|
| **A**cidosis | pH <7.1–7.15 refractory |
| **E**lectrolytes | K >6.5 or ECG changes refractory |
| **I**ntoxication | Methanol, ethylene glycol, lithium, salicylates, metformin (lactic acidosis), valproate |
| **O**verload | Pulmonary edema not responding to diuretics |
| **U**remia | Pericarditis, encephalopathy, bleeding |

:::pearl
**Contrast:** IV iodinated contrast risk is low when eGFR ≥30. eGFR <30 → isotonic fluids pre/post if not overloaded. Do not delay a needed CT.
:::

## ⚠️ Pitfalls

- No bladder scan in an old man on anticholinergics.
- "Prerenal" label for days while ATN develops.
- Missing **rhabdo** (CK) or **abdominal compartment syndrome** (bladder pressure).
- Forgetting to re-dose meds when kidney recovers.

## 🎓 Board corner

| Stem | Answer |
|---|---|
| AKI + rash + eosinophilia after PPI | AIN → stop PPI, consider steroids |
| AKI + hemoptysis + RBC casts | Pulmonary-renal syndrome → ANCA/anti-GBM, nephrology now |
| Cr up after starting lisinopril, FENa 0.4% | Hemodynamic (ACEi) — hold if large rise |
| Muddy brown casts after hypotension | ATN — supportive |
| Envelope-shaped crystals + AG acidosis | Ethylene glycol → fomepizole, dialysis |
