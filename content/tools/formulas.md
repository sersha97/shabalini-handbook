## 🧪 Acid–base

| Formula | Equation | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Anion gap | Na − (Cl + HCO₃) | Normal ~8–12 |
| Albumin-corrected AG | AG + 2.5 × (4 − albumin) | Low albumin hides a gap |
| Delta–delta | (AG − 12) ÷ (24 − HCO₃) | <1 + NAGMA · >2 + met alkalosis |
| Winter's | PaCO₂ = 1.5 × HCO₃ + 8 ± 2 | Metabolic acidosis compensation |
| Metabolic alkalosis | PaCO₂ ≈ 0.7 × HCO₃ + 21 ± 2 | Compensation |
| Osmolar gap | Measured − (2Na + Glu/18 + BUN/2.8 + EtOH/3.7) | >10 → toxic alcohol |
| Urine anion gap | UNa + UK − UCl | Negative = GI · Positive = RTA |

## 🫘 Kidney & electrolytes

| Formula | Equation | Use |
|---|---|---|
| FENa | (UNa × PCr) ÷ (PNa × UCr) × 100 | <1% prerenal · >2% ATN |
| FEUrea | (UUrea × PCr) ÷ (PUrea × UCr) × 100 | <35% prerenal (on diuretics) |
| Cockcroft–Gault | (140 − age) × kg ÷ (72 × Cr) × 0.85 if female | Drug dosing |
| Corrected Na | Na + 1.6 × (glucose − 100)/100 | Hyperglycemia |
| Effective osmolality | 2 × Na + glucose/18 | HHS (>300) |
| Corrected Ca | Ca + 0.8 × (4 − albumin) | Prefer ionized Ca |
| Free water deficit | TBW × (Na/140 − 1) | Hypernatremia |
| TBW | 0.6 × kg (men) · 0.5 (women, older men) · 0.45 (older women) | |
| Adrogué–Madias | (infusate Na + K − serum Na) ÷ (TBW + 1) | Na change per 1 L |

## 🫁 Lungs

| Formula | Equation | Use |
|---|---|---|
| A–a gradient | [FiO₂ × 713 − PaCO₂/0.8] − PaO₂ | Normal ≈ age/4 + 4 |
| P/F ratio | PaO₂ ÷ FiO₂ | ARDS ≤300 / ≤200 / ≤100 |
| S/F ratio | SpO₂ ÷ FiO₂ | ARDS ≤315 (2023 definition) |
| ROX | (SpO₂/FiO₂) ÷ RR | HFNC success ≥4.88 |
| Predicted body weight | Men 50 + 2.3 × (in − 60) · Women 45.5 + 2.3 × (in − 60) | Tidal volume |
| Driving pressure | Plateau − PEEP | Goal <15 |

## ❤️ Heart

| Formula | Equation | Use |
|---|---|---|
| MAP | DBP + (SBP − DBP)/3 | Goal ≥65 |
| QTc Bazett | QT ÷ √RR | Over-corrects at high HR |
| QTc Fridericia | QT ÷ ∛RR | Better at HR <60 / >90 |
| Cardiac power output | MAP × CO ÷ 451 | <0.6 W = high-risk shock |
| CHA₂DS₂-VASc | See AF page | Stroke risk |
| RCRI | See pre-op page | Peri-op MACE |
