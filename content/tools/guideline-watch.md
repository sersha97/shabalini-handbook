## 🆕 Recent changes reflected in this handbook

| Date | Guideline | What changed | Pages |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jul 2026 | **ACC ECDP — HFpEF** | SGLT2i + **finerenone** backbone; GLP-1 RA / tirzepatide if BMI ≥30; β-blockers only with an indication | HFpEF |
| Jun 2026 | **Second Universal Definition of HF** | Less reliance on rigid EF cut-offs; cause and trajectory; new AHA/ACC HF guideline expected 2027 | HFrEF, HFpEF |
| Mar 2026 | **Surviving Sepsis Campaign 2026** | NEWS/MEWS/SIRS over qSOFA; balanced crystalloids; prolonged-infusion β-lactams; MAP 60–65 if ≥65 y; no routine anaerobic coverage | Sepsis, Hypotension, Fever |
| Jan 2026 | **AHA/ASA Acute Ischemic Stroke** | **Tenecteplase 0.25 mg/kg** = alteplase as first line; EVT expanded | Stroke |
| Jul 2025 | **IDSA complicated UTI** | Afebrile cystitis in men = uncomplicated; 7-day courses; early oral switch | UTI |
| Jul 2025 | **ATS CAP update** | Steroids for **severe** CAP, not non-severe; short courses; lung ultrasound; viral-positive inpatients | CAP |
| 2025 | **AHA ACLS** | AF and flutter cardioversion start **200 J** | AF, Flutter, Quick cards |
| Feb 2025 | **ACC/AHA ACS** | Ticagrelor/prasugrel preferred; LDL goal <70 (consider <55); complete revascularization in stable STEMI | STEMI, NSTE-ACS |
| 2024 | **ADA/EASD hyperglycemic crises** | New DKA/HHS criteria (glucose ≥200 or known DM; β-OHB ≥3.0) | DKA/HHS |
| 2024 | **ACC/AHA peri-operative** | BNP/NT-proBNP for risk; SGLT2i hold 3 days | Pre-op |
