## ⚡ Synchronized cardioversion

| Rhythm | Mode | First shock (biphasic) |
|---|---|---|
| Atrial fibrillation | Sync | **≥200 J** (AHA 2025) |
| Atrial flutter | Sync | **200 J** (AHA 2025) |
| Narrow regular (SVT) | Sync | 100 J |
| Monomorphic VT with pulse | Sync | 100 J |
| Polymorphic VT / VF / pulseless | **Unsync** (defibrillate) | Device dose (120–200 J) or max |

1. Consent if possible. Monitor, O₂, suction, airway kit.
2. Sedate: etomidate 0.1–0.15 mg/kg or small propofol doses.
3. Pads anterior–posterior (AF).
4. Press **SYNC** — check a marker on every R wave. Re-press after each shock (many devices reset).
5. Charge → "All clear" → press and hold until it fires.
6. After: ECG, BP, anticoagulation plan.

## 💨 Adenosine

- 6 mg rapid IV push + 20 mL flush → 12 mg → 12 mg. 3 mg if central line/transplant/dipyridamole.
- Record a strip. Warn the patient.
- ⛔ Irregular or wide tachycardia, severe bronchospasm.

## 🫁 Rapid sequence intubation

- Pre-oxygenate 3 min; nasal cannula 15 L during attempt.
- **Resuscitate before you intubate** (push-dose pressor ready).
- Induction: **etomidate 0.3 mg/kg** · **ketamine 1–2 mg/kg** (shock, asthma) · propofol 1–2 mg/kg (drops BP).
- Paralytic: **rocuronium 1.2 mg/kg** · succinylcholine 1.5 mg/kg (⛔ hyperK, burns/crush >24 h, NM disease, MH).
- Confirm with waveform EtCO₂. Start sedation right away.

## 🎛️ Initial ventilator settings

| Setting | Start |
|---|---|
| Mode | AC / volume |
| Tidal volume | 6–8 mL/kg PBW (6 in ARDS) |
| Rate | 16–20 (24–30 if severe metabolic acidosis) |
| PEEP | 5–8 |
| FiO₂ | 100% → wean to SpO₂ 92–96% |

Check ABG at 30 min · plateau <30 · driving pressure <15. Obstructive disease: rate 10–14, long expiration.

## 😷 BiPAP

- Best for **COPD + hypercapnic acidosis**, **cardiogenic pulmonary edema**.
- Start IPAP 10–12 / EPAP 5. CO₂ high → ↑ IPAP. O₂ low → ↑ EPAP/FiO₂.
- ABG at 1–2 h. No better → intubate.
- ⛔ Cannot protect airway, vomiting, GI bleed, facial trauma, shock.

## 🌬️ High-flow nasal cannula

- Flow 40–60 L/min, FiO₂ to SpO₂ 92–96%.
- **ROX** ≥4.88 → success likely · <3.85 → prepare to intubate.

## 💉 Vasopressors

| Drug | Range | When |
|---|---|---|
| **Norepinephrine** | 0.05–0.5 mcg/kg/min | First line |
| Vasopressin | 0.03 U/min fixed | Add at NE ~0.25 |
| Epinephrine | 0.05–0.5 mcg/kg/min | 3rd agent, anaphylaxis, low CO |
| Phenylephrine | 0.5–4 mcg/kg/min | Tachyarrhythmia; LVOT obstruction |
| Dobutamine | 2.5–10 mcg/kg/min | Low cardiac output |

- Peripheral NE OK short-term (good forearm IV, check site). Extravasation → phentolamine.
- Push-dose: phenylephrine 100 mcg · epinephrine 10–20 mcg.

## 🧂 Stress-dose steroids

- **Septic shock on pressors:** hydrocortisone 50 mg IV q6h (± fludrocortisone 50 mcg).
- **Adrenal crisis:** hydrocortisone 100 mg IV → 50 mg q6h.
- **Severe CAP:** hydrocortisone 200 mg/day × 4–7 days.
